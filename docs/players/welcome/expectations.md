## Attitude and behaviour

We are a relaxed community and enjoy plenty of jokes and banter, but with the caveat that everyone should be treated with respect. Discrimination, harassment, personal attacks and deliberate disruption and/or hostile behaviour will not be tolerated.

## Preparation and participation

You do not need to attend every operation. We appreciate more than most groups that real life always comes first.

However, if you sign up, please make a reasonable effort to arrive on time, with the correct mods installed and working. If you are going to be late, or need to leave early, let someone know where possible.

Before joining, check both the Discord sign-up and read any provided briefing document.

During an operation, we expect players to remain involved and avoid behaviour that distracts from the mission or disrupts other players.

## Teamwork

Our operations are built around cooperation. Stay with your assigned team or squad, communicate useful information, and support the plan being followed by your team.

Initiative is encouraged when it helps the group, but not when it means abandoning your role, ignoring instructions, or acting alone at the expense of other players. If you are unsure, try and ask.

Leadership roles should provide clear direction and listen to their palyer. Other players should follow reasonable instructions, and make an effort to carry out orders promptly and efficiently.

## Giving and receiving feedback

Many improvements to our missions, framework, mods and community rules have come directly from player feedback. We encourage everyone to share their thoughts, provided that feedback is:

1. Give at an appropriate time - not during an operation unless the issue needs immediate attention.
2. Respectful and focused on the problem rather than the person.
3. Constructive, explaining what could be improved, and suggesting an alternative where possible.

## What new players do not need to know

You do not need extensive Arma experience, any military knowledge, or familiarity with every mod before joining us.

New players can begin in a straightforward role and learn through playing. More experienced members are onhand to help with setup, controls and unfamiliar systems both in and out of missions.

Leadership and specialist roles may require additional knowledge. While we do not have hard training requirements to take these roles, you should feel comfortable and confident assuming these responsibilities before taking them.

Ask questions, be willing to learn and work with the people around you - that is more important than arriving as an expert.
