# Redeployment
Rejoin your side without disrupting the operation.
</div>

## Typical flow

<div class="jmf-step-grid" markdown>

1. :material-map-search: **Open** the redeployment interface.
2. :material-filter-variant: **Choose** an available friendly destination.
3. :material-check-circle: **Confirm** and wait for deployment.
4. :material-account-group: **Regroup** with your element.

</div>

Destinations may include squad rallies, a platoon rally, command vehicles or eligible squadmates. Availability depends on mission settings, your side and the current battlefield state.

!!! warning "Before deploying"
    Confirm the destination is still safe and appropriate. Follow leadership instructions if reinforcements are being grouped.
