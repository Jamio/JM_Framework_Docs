# Operations and Scheduling
How to find upcoming operations and let us know you are attending.
</div>

<!-- Optional header image: docs/assets/images/player-operations.jpg -->

## Finding an operation

Operations are announced as **Discord events using Apollo**. Each event includes the date, start time, and a brief summary of the mission.

The bot will adjust the start time to your local time zone, so there is no need to convert.

## Registering your attendance

Use the response options on the Discord event to show whether you expect to attend:

- ✅ **Accepted** — you intend to attend.
- ❔ **Tentative** — you may be able to attend but cannot confirm yet.
- ❌ **Declined** — you do not expect to attend.

Responses help the Mission Maker plan roles, vehicles and player numbers. They are not rigid commitments, but please update yours if your plans change.

## When to arrive

Join the appropriate Discord voice channel a few minutes before the advertised start time. 

!!! info "At a glance"
    - **Arrive:** 15 mins before advertised start time
    - **Typical duration:** 2 - 4 hrs
    - **Before joining:** Check the event, briefing and mod preset

!!! note "Allow time for setup"
    Slotting, technical issues and the in-game briefing may mean the operation itself begins shortly after the advertised start time.

## If your plans change

Real life takes priority. If you can no longer attend, update your response on the event whenever possible. If you expect to arrive late or leave early, let someone know if possible.
