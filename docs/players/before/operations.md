# Operations and Scheduling
How to find upcoming operations, register your interest and arrive ready to play.
</div>

## Where operations are announced

Upcoming operations are announced as Discord events using Apollo. Each event should include the mission date, start time, required mod preset and a short description of the operation.

More detailed information—such as the full briefing, available roles or special instructions—may be posted separately and linked from the event.

Check the event before joining, particularly if it has been updated since you first registered.

## Showing interest or signing up

Use the response options on the Discord event to show whether you expect to attend:

- **Accepted** — you intend to attend.
- **Tentative** — you may attend but cannot confirm yet.
- **Declined** — you do not expect to attend.

Registering helps the Mission Maker plan player numbers, roles, vehicles and the scale of the mission. You are still welcome to join if you did not register in advance, provided space is available.

If you select **Accepted**, please update your response if your plans later change.

## Start times and duration

All event times are displayed through Discord and should automatically appear in your local time zone.

The advertised start time is when we aim to begin organising players and preparing to launch the mission. Please join the appropriate Discord voice channel a few minutes beforehand so there is time to resolve mod problems and assign any remaining roles.

Operations will vary in length, but most are expected to last approximately **[add typical duration]**. Mission Makers should mention unusually short or long sessions in the event description.

!!! note "Allow time for setup"
    Slotting, technical issues and the in-game briefing may mean the operation itself begins shortly after the advertised start time.

## If your plans change

Real life takes priority. If you can no longer attend, update your response on the event whenever possible.

If you expect to arrive late or leave early, let the Mission Maker or a member of the mission leadership know. This is particularly important if you have requested a leadership, vehicle crew or specialist role that may need to be reassigned.

There is no need for a lengthy explanation—a short message is enough to help us plan around your absence.

## Joining in progress

Joining an operation after it has started may be possible, depending on the mission and its available reinforcement systems.

If you arrive late:

1. Join the Arma and Teamspeak servers
2. Check whether the operation is still accepting additional players.
3. Select an unassigned slot.
4. Follow the instructions provided for spawning or redeploying.

Do not select an empty slot without first checking that it does not belong to a disconnected or late player. This information should be in the briefing document.

!!! warning "JIP availability"
    Joining in progress cannot be guaranteed. Some missions may restrict late joining for technical, balance or narrative reasons. This is not a normal situation, but it is worth being aware of.
