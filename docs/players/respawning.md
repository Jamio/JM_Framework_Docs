## Player essentials

- The mission may use immediate respawn, timed waves, tickets or permanent death.
- Do not respawn unless the mission system or leadership permits it.
- Your previous role, loadout or group may be restored automatically.
- After returning, follow the mission's [Redeployment](redeployment.md) procedure.

    The briefing should explain the active respawn model. This page will document the final v1 interface and restrictions.
