# In-Game Briefing
The final information given before the operation begins.
</div>

## Listen for five things

<div class="jmf-step-grid" markdown>

1. :material-target: **Objective** — what your team must achieve.
2. :material-account-multiple: **Organisation** — your group, leader and callsign.
3. :material-map-marker-path: **Opening plan** — where you are going first.
4. :material-radio: **Communications** — radio channels and checks.
5. :material-alert-circle: **Changes** — anything different from the written briefing.

</div>

The in-game briefing confirms the plan and covers last-minute changes. It does not normally repeat the full written [Mission Briefing](before/briefings.md).

!!! question "Something unclear?"
    Ask before step-off. Keep the question short and relevant to what your character or role should know.
