## Basic use

1. Approach the garage or synced interaction object.
2. Open the available garage action.
3. Choose an authorised vehicle for your side.
4. Confirm the spawn area is clear.
5. Prepare the vehicle before leaving.

!!! note "Mission configuration varies"
    Vehicle lists, quantities, servicing and customisation are controlled per garage. This page will be updated when the v1 interface is final.
