# Unknown Weapons

Unknown Weapons penalizes firearms outside the player's approved Arsenal/loadout whitelist by increasing dispersion, ACE jamming and reload-failure risk.

Enable and tune it under Optional Systems. Whitelists are assembled from shared Arsenal definitions, side-specific definitions, role loadouts and explicit extra classnames. Thrown grenades and placed explosives are excluded.

The first shot from an unfamiliar weapon shows one explanatory notification. Subsequent ACE jam/reload feedback is left to ACE itself.
