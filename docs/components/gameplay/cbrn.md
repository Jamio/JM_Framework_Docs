# CBRN

CBRN is a contamination, diagnosis, treatment and decontamination system built around ACE interactions and medical treatment.

## Threats and protection

Threat Zones define Chemical, Biological, Radiological or Custom contamination. Configure subtype, radius, falloff, exposure rate, protection requirement, containment and particle preset. Respirators, protective suits and supplied-air backpacks contribute protection levels.

## Diagnosis and treatment

Players receive vague symptom descriptions; qualified medics can use a Bioassay Kit for exact type, subtype, severity and treatment burden. Diagnosis improves treatment effectiveness. Subtype-specific items treat chemical injury, infection or radiological contamination, while the Experimental Treatment Serum covers a configured Custom agent.

## Progression

Exposure advances through severity stages. Later stages may cause ACE pain, visual distortion, vomiting, collapse, unconsciousness and death. Biological agents can progress after incubation and transmit between nearby people.

## Decontamination

Personal kits and Decontamination Stations clear contamination, symptoms and contagious state without replacing subtype treatment. Zeus tools create threats/stations, modify exposure and inspect patients.
