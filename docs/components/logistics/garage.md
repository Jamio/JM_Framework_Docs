# Garage

Garage provides vehicle deployment, servicing and appearance customisation through themed dialogs.

Place a Garage module, configure its vehicle definitions and synchronize it to an access object. Add Ground, Air or Naval Spawn Point modules for every vehicle family in the catalogue. Repair, refuel, rearm and customisation services can be enabled independently with range and safety requirements.

Spawning and servicing are validated on the server. Zeus can add/remove access objects and service vehicles. Access actions rebuild for JIP clients and after live reconfiguration.
