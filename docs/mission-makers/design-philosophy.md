# Design Philosophy
Build around player decisions, readable objectives and recoverable mission flow.
</div>

<div class="grid cards jmf-feature-cards" markdown>

-   :material-account-multiple: **Players first**

    Design what players will understand, decide and experience.

-   :material-target: **Clear purpose**

    Every task should contribute to the mission's central idea.

-   :material-routes: **Flexible flow**

    Expect delay, casualties, missed triggers and unexpected choices.

-   :material-puzzle-check: **Framework supports design**

    Enable systems because the mission needs them—not because they exist.

</div>

## Quick design questions

1. What is the operation really about?
2. What information do players receive?
3. What meaningful decisions can they make?
4. What happens if the first plan fails?
5. What must be tested in multiplayer?
