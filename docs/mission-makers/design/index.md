## The mission design brief

<!-- Add a short reusable planning template or link to the group's planning document. -->

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- **Context and briefing**  
  Give players enough information to act without removing uncertainty.

- **Objectives and flow**  
  Create clear goals, useful choices, and recoverable progression.

- **Players and loadouts**  
  Match roles, equipment, and support to the experience.

- **Opposition and challenge**  
  Design credible resistance without relying on raw unit count.

- **Win and fail conditions**  
  Decide what success means and how the mission ends cleanly.
